Rightree

Native plants expected at a site, from where they grow in similar places nearby.

Draw an area or load a file to start.
  1. Draw your site on the map, or load a KML exported from Occurd.
  2. Press Predict. It takes about 10 seconds.
  3. Each part of the site gets lists for three stages: mature forest, regenerating (kānuka, scrub, young hardwoods) and open ground, plus a wetland list where there is one.

Close matches are good evidence. Best available means no close matches, so treat the list as a lead to check.
