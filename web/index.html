Rightree

Native plants expected at a site, from where they grow in similar places nearby.

Draw an area with the polygon or rectangle tool on the map, or load a file.
  1. Draw your site with the tools at the top left of the map, or load a KML exported from Occurd.
  2. Press Predict.
  3. If the site holds distinct habitats it is split into units, mapped and described. Each unit gets lists for mature forest, regenerating (kānuka, scrub, young hardwoods) and open ground, plus a wetland list where there is one.

Close matches are good evidence. Best available means no close matches, so treat the list as a lead to check.
