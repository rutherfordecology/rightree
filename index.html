  1. Draw your site with the tools at the top left of the map, or load a KML, KMZ or GeoJSON file.
  2. Press Predict.
  3. If the site holds distinct habitats it is split into units, mapped and described. Each unit gets lists for mature forest, regenerating (kānuka, scrub, young hardwoods) and open ground, plus a wetland list where there is one.

Close matches are good evidence. Best available means no close matches, so treat the list as a lead to check.

Welcome to Rightree

Rightree lists the native plants expected at a site, from where they grow in similar places nearby.

The model runs in your browser, so nothing you draw leaves your computer. To do that it downloads about 45 MB once (Python and its maths libraries), which your browser keeps for next time. Each site then loads a few MB of map data.

On a mobile connection you may want to wait for Wi-Fi.
